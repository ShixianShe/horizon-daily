## [BDH-CQ: Recurrent Latent Reasoning for In-Context Learning](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vov5r5/bdhcq_incontext_learning_with_recurrent_latent/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers introduced BDH-CQ, a 150M-parameter reasoning system that combines in-context learning with recurrent latent reasoning, achieving 29.5% pass@2 on ARC-AGI-1 at a cost of $0.00070 per task, breaking the cost-accuracy Pareto frontier. This work demonstrates that efficient, low-cost reasoning systems can achieve competitive performance on challenging benchmarks like ARC-AGI-1, potentially shifting research focus toward more resource-efficient AI architectures. It also highlights the promise of latent reasoning as an alternative to verbose chain-of-thought approaches. BDH-CQ updates its recurrent memory with demonstrations of unseen tasks at inference time, then solves queries through iterative computation in a high-dimensional latent space without decoding intermediate reasoning steps. The model does not use task identifiers or evaluation-task demonstration pairs during training, and no parameters are updated at inference time.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 15, 06:18

**Background**: ARC-AGI is a benchmark designed to measure general intelligence through fluid, systematic, and few-shot generalization across diverse tasks, emphasizing 'easy for humans, hard for AI.' In-context learning allows models to adapt to new tasks from examples provided at inference time, while recurrent latent reasoning performs computation in a hidden state space rather than generating explicit language. BDH-CQ combines these ideas to achieve efficient adaptation and reasoning.

## [Abdominal Fat Outperforms BMI in Predicting Heart Disease Risk](https://www.acc.org/about-acc/press-releases/2026/08/11/14/59/abdominal-fat-predicts-heart-disease-risk-better-than-bmi) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A new study presented by the American College of Cardiology reveals that abdominal (visceral) fat, measured via waist circumference or waist-to-hip ratio, is a better predictor of cardiovascular disease risk than body mass index (BMI). The findings were based on data from over 260,000 individuals followed for about 20 years. This finding challenges the widespread reliance on BMI as a primary health metric, potentially leading to more accurate risk assessments and personalized interventions. It could influence clinical guidelines and public health strategies, encouraging the use of waist measurements in routine screenings. The study compared BMI, waist circumference (WC), and waist-to-hip ratio (WHR) against nine cardiovascular and mortality outcomes. Notably, BMI failed to account for fat distribution, leading to misclassification of risk in some individuals. The research highlights the importance of visceral fat, which surrounds internal organs, as opposed to subcutaneous fat.

hackernews · theanonymousone · Aug 15, 21:14 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49314403)

**Background**: BMI is a simple metric calculated from height and weight, but it does not distinguish between muscle and fat or account for fat distribution. Visceral fat is metabolically active and linked to inflammation and cardiovascular disease. Waist circumference and waist-to-hip ratio are inexpensive and easy-to-measure proxies for visceral fat, making them practical alternatives in clinical settings.

## [Jacobian Lens Transfers Across Qwen Versions Without Refitting](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vpa5cv/survival_of_the_fitted_qwen3627bs_jacobian_lens/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A Reddit user tested whether a Jacobian lens fitted to Qwen3.6-27B transfers to Qwen3.8-27B without refitting, finding it still reads latent entities accurately. The lens survived the version update, with the transferred readout keeping target entities near the top of the vocabulary and steering directions still working. This is significant because interpretability lenses are typically fitted to a specific checkpoint, and it was unknown whether they survive model updates. The finding suggests that monitoring pipelines may not need to refit lenses on every release, saving computational resources and enabling more practical interpretability deployment. The test used 40 two-hop prompts where the middle entity is never stated, and the transferred lens kept the latent entity at median rank 4 at layer 48 on the home model versus 17 transferred, with the successor actually better at layer 24 (rank 121 vs 38). Steering directions for 'paradox' derived from the old checkpoint still suppressed the concept in the new model's outputs, while WikiText next-token prediction showed transfer costs of 1.2-1.3x mid-network and about 2x by layer 48.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/imstilllearningthis · Aug 15, 18:24

**Background**: The Jacobian lens is an interpretability technique introduced by Anthropic in a July 2026 paper, which uses the Jacobian of the model to read latent 'global workspace' representations. It is related to the logit lens, which decodes internal activations into vocabulary probabilities, but the Jacobian lens captures more abstract, entity-level information. The test was conducted on Qwen3.6-27B and Qwen3.8-27B, which share the same architecture and tokenizer, making transfer comparison clean.

A new at-home test kit called LymeAlert, priced around $50, is designed to detect Borrelia burgdorferi, the pathogen causing Lyme disease, directly in ticks. The kit includes a 'Tick Crusher' to pulverize the tick and remains effective for up to 12 months. This innovation could improve early Lyme disease diagnosis by enabling people to test ticks they find on themselves or pets, potentially leading to faster treatment. However, experts question its accuracy and the lack of FDA oversight, which could lead to false reassurance or unnecessary anxiety. The test is a lateral flow test, which has a much higher limit of detection compared to molecular tests like PCR, meaning it may miss low-level infections. Tick tests do not require FDA clearance, so the manufacturer's claims of 'lab-level accuracy' are likely unreviewed.

## [CORS Chat: A Browser-Based Tool for Testing OpenAI-Compatible Endpoints](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/15/cors-chat/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Simon Willison released CORS Chat, a web UI for testing OpenAI-Responses-compatible chat endpoints with CORS support. It features browser-persisted conversations, JSON export, and progressive SVG rendering during token streaming. This tool simplifies the testing of local and remote LLM endpoints that support CORS, making it easier for developers to experiment with models like Qwen 3.8 27B in LM Studio or via OpenRouter. Its progressive SVG rendering showcases a practical way to visualize streaming outputs, which could inspire similar features in other chat UIs. CORS Chat works with LM Studio's --cors option and OpenRouter, and it persists conversations in the browser with copy-paste JSON export. A notable detail is that it detects SVG images being generated and progressively renders them in the chat while tokens are still streaming.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 15, 14:49

**Background**: The OpenAI Responses API is a modern endpoint for chat completions that supports streaming and persistent conversations, and many tools like LM Studio and OpenRouter offer OpenAI-compatible interfaces. CORS (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing) is a browser security mechanism that allows web pages to make requests to different domains, which is essential for web-based tools to interact with local or remote API endpoints. Progressive rendering is a technique where content is displayed incrementally as it is received, improving perceived performance and user experience.
