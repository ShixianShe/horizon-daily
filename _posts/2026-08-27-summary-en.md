This milestone significantly improves the usability of Linux on Apple Silicon M3 Macs, making them more viable as daily drivers for developers and enthusiasts. ## [575k Manual Crop Labels Recovered to Automate Book Digitization; Scaling Fails, 10 Clicks Beat It](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vz2ojw/we_recovered_575k_crop_labels_from_a_decade_of/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A decade of manual Photoshop crop labels from the Ibteda Digital Library were recovered and used to train models for automated book digitization, but scaling data, model size, and resolution did not improve performance. Ten operator-corrected crops per book outperformed all scaling levers, raising pass@80 from 0.71 to 0.83. This work challenges common assumptions in machine learning that more data and larger models always help, highlighting the importance of human-in-the-loop and domain-specific constraints. It provides a practical example for archival digitization and opens discussion on calibrating models to invisible human preferences. The negative results showed that scaling from 378 to 572 training books, using ResNet-50, 1024px inputs, or a spatial head did not improve unseen-book pass@80. Per-book error analysis revealed near-constant offsets per volume, which are not present in the pixels of a new book, explaining why calibration examples are effective.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/laamaleph · Aug 26, 16:53

**Background**: SIFT (Scale-Invariant Feature Transform) is a computer vision algorithm for detecting and matching local features, used here to register finished pages back to raw photos. MAGSAC is a robust model fitting algorithm that does not require a single inlier-outlier threshold, used for conservative acceptance gates. Pass@80 is a metric measuring the probability that a model produces a correct output within 80 attempts, commonly used in code generation but adapted here for crop accuracy.

## [Nepal Glacier Collapse Triggers Deadly Flash Flood](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02716-w) ⭐️ 7.0/10

On 26 August 2026, an ice-and-rock avalanche from a glacier above the Bhotekoshi River system on the Nepal-China border dammed a tributary, the Lhende Khola, before the blockage gave way, sending a surge of water and debris downstream and causing a deadly flash flood in the Rasuwa and Nuwakot districts. This event highlights how global warming destabilizes high-altitude regions, potentially leading to more frequent glacier collapses and associated floods. It underscores the urgent need for improved monitoring and early warning systems in vulnerable mountain communities, particularly in the Himalayas where millions are at risk. The flood was caused by a glacier collapse that dammed a tributary, creating a temporary lake that then burst—a mechanism similar to a glacial lake outburst flood (GLOF). The event occurred in a region with active geology, and a 2023 study found 15 million people at risk from such hazards, mostly in China, India, Nepal, Pakistan, and Peru.

rss · Nature · Aug 27, 00:00

**Background**: Glacier collapse occurs when a glacier becomes unstable, often due to increased meltwater from warming temperatures, leading to sudden ice and rock avalanches. Such collapses can block rivers, creating temporary dams that may fail catastrophically, releasing large volumes of water downstream—a phenomenon known as a glacial lake outburst flood (GLOF). Climate change is accelerating glacial melting and permafrost thaw, increasing the frequency and severity of these events in high-mountain regions like the Himalayas.

**Background**: Underground research is critical for geology, mining engineering, and other fields, but many countries have laws or regulations that restrict women from entering mines, often based on outdated assumptions about physical strength or safety. These restrictions are increasingly challenged as more women pursue careers in STEM. Modernizing safety regulations involves risk assessment that considers actual hazards rather than gender, potentially allowing women to participate while maintaining safety standards.
